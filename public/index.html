Frequently Asked Questions (Karachi & Nationwide)

How does NURAH by TY fabric quality compare to brands like Khaadi, Nishat, and Sapphire? +

NURAH by TY (Nurah by TY / Al Anwar Clothes) is based directly inside Karachi's legendary textile epicenter — Iqbal Cloth Market. We source directly from the same tier-1 spinning and printing mills that produce for Pakistan's top luxury fashion houses. Our fabrics utilize high-density 80/80 and 90/70 combed cotton yarns, colorfast digital reactive printing that resists bleeding, and opulent hand-detailed sequin, thread, and tilla embroidery. You get the identical or superior drape, softness, and durability of high-end brands without paying high mall brand premiums.

Absolutely. We are an authorized wholesale stockist and manufacturer partner.
